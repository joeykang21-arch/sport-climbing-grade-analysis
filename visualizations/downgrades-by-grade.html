Which routes moved down while each grade held the ceiling

The same windows as the runway page — each era runs from the breakthrough that opened it to the one that closed it — but a different question. Not what was established at the top grade, but which routes climbed during the era were later judged easier than the first ascentionist said: 29 of them, at every grade, not only at the ceiling.

A route is placed by when it was climbed, not by when opinion turned, because the turn has no date in the record. Its move is read as the first ascentionist's suggestion against today's consensus. That field is sparse at the source, so every count here is a floor, not a census — and an era with none recorded is kept on the page rather than dropped, because the gap is in the record, not in the climbing.

Both directions, era by era

57 up and 29 down across the ten eras, 14 of them at the frontier

The lighter part of each bar is the moves that touched the era’s own top grade — an upgrade that arrived at the ceiling, or a downgrade claimed at or above it and then cut back. Only 14 of the 86 moves qualify, 7 of them in the 9a+ era alone; since 9b the frontier has taken none at all, and every recorded move has been in the backlog below it.

Counts, not rates: the eras run from 2 mo (8b) to 12.7 yrs (9a+), so a tall bar can mean a long era as much as a busy one. Both arms share one scale, and the lighter parts sit on the gutter so they can be compared across eras. Click a band for that era’s routes; hover for the split.
