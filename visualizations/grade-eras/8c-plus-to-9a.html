The 8c+ era, 1990–1990

Opened by Liquid Ambar (Jerry Moffatt, 1990) — the first 8c+ by today's consensus.

This era lasted just 11 weeks — one of the shortest in the sport’s history, and an artefact of consensus: both routes were first climbed in 1990 and regraded later.

One dot per first ascent at the era’s top grade; the blue dot is the route that opened the era. Hover a dot for the route.
