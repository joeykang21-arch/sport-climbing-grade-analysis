Which boulder problems moved while each grade held the ceiling

The sport question, asked of the boulder control group. Each era runs from the first problem at a Font grade to the first problem at the next (8A → 8A+ → 8B → 8B+ → 8C → 8C+ → 9A → 9A+), and a problem is counted in the era it was climbed in, not the one its final grade belongs to. Its move is the first ascentionist’s suggestion read against today’s consensus: 214 problems settled away from what their own first ascentionist said, and 203 of them fall inside an era window — 83 upward and 120 downward. The 11 left out have no usable first-ascent date, or predate the 8A ceiling, so there is no era to put them in.

The boulder record is the better populated of the two. The site carries an FA suggestion for enough problems to put 203 moves on this chart against 86 on the sport pages — and the balance runs the other way, downgrades clearly ahead of upgrades where sport leans up. Both are floors rather than censuses: a problem with no recorded suggestion cannot appear, and that is a fact about the record rather than about the climbing.

Both directions, era by era

83 up and 120 down across the 8 eras, 23 of them at the frontier

The lighter part of each bar is the moves that touched the era’s own top grade — an upgrade that arrived at the ceiling, or a downgrade claimed at or above it and then cut back. Only 23 of the 203 moves qualify, 7 of them downgrades in the 8C era alone; nearly all boulder regrading happens in the backlog below the frontier, which is the shape the sport pages show too. The 8A and 8A+ bands are empty because the suggested-grade field is effectively unpopulated that early, not because nothing moved.

Counts, not rates: the dated eras run from 10 mo (9A+) to 17.0 yrs (8B), so a tall bar can mean a long era as much as a busy one. Both arms share one scale, and the lighter parts sit on the gutter so they can be compared across eras. Hover a band for the split.

8A has no measurable span: Meathook (8A) and Trice (8A+) are both recorded as 1975 and nothing finer, so both anchor to 1 January and the era between them closes the day it opens. The band is kept rather than dropped, because the missing span is missing from the record, not from the history.
