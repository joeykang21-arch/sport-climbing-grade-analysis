How much exists at a grade before the next one arrives

For each grade, every route established at it before the first route of the next grade — the runway. The count is the headline; the routes are what the count is made of, which is why three at 9b+ reads differently from eighteen at 9a+.

Grades are today's consensus, so a route counts on the runway of the grade it holds now, not the one it was given. Disputed claims are drawn and labelled rather than dropped: they stood on the runway at the time, and removing them would quietly change a count.
